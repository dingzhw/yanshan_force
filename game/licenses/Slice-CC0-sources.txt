Tabasco, Various Small Arms (M4 geometry)
https://opengameart.org/node/3609
Author page lists CC0 and an older CC-BY 3.0 entry; the author explicitly placed
the assets in the public domain in the 2023-12-15 update.
Original public file: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/smallarms.zip
Modification: selected m4.blend, normalized scale/axes, converted material slots
to PBR, bevelled edges, exported static GLB. New view animation and hands are local.

Kless Gyzen, Rock Moss Set 01
https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_moss_set_01
Modification: 1K diffuse/normal/roughness textures, six individual rocks exported
as GLB, simplified to approximately 1800 polygons per rock before triangulation.

Jack Mava, Old Military Crate
https://polyhaven.com/a/old_military_crate
Modification: selected variant A, rebuilt PBR material from 1K source channels,
exported GLB. The local interaction supplies crate-opening and reward state.

Poly Haven, Pine Tree 01
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01
Only 1K bark diffuse, twig diffuse and twig alpha textures are used. Local
lightweight trunks/foliage placement replace the heavy original tree geometry.
License details for all Poly Haven assets: https://polyhaven.com/license

ambientCG / Lennart Demes, Ground 015
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground015
Modification: selected 1K color, OpenGL normal and roughness textures; the local
terrain shader blends paths and forest floor. Other source maps are not shipped.
License: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Smoke Mountain / Yanshan v0.5.2 first-person tactical arms additions (Retrieved 2026-11-12, CC0 1.0):

Blender Studio, Human Base Meshes Bundle v1.4.1 (GEO-body_male_realistic)
https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/
Original public file: https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip
Modification: evaluated Multires Level 1 sculpted male arms and hands in Blender 4.5,
trimmed outside shoulder armpits, tailored BDU ripstop sleeves, half-finger tactical
gloves, carbon knuckle guards, and 3D left-wrist telemetry watch, rigged to a 39-bone
armature with 5 weapon-grip / ADS / reload / trigger poses, exported as fp_arms.glb.

ambientCG / Lennart Demes, Fabric 030 & Leather 026
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric030
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Leather026
Modification: resampled 1K cloth weave and leather grain maps into 512x512 olive-drab
ripstop, woodland camouflage, matte tactical glove, and skin pore PBR channels.
License: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/
